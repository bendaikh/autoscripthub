# Landing Page Builder - Quick Start Guide

## ⚡ 5-Minute Setup

### Step 1: Access the Feature
1. Login to admin panel
2. Look for **"Landing Pages"** in the sidebar menu
3. Click to view all landing pages

### Step 2: Create Your First Landing Page
1. Click **"Add Landing Page"** button (top right)
2. Fill in the form:

#### Required Fields:
- **Page Title**: "My Awesome Product"
- **Description**: Write at least 2-3 paragraphs describing your product
- **Price**: Enter your product price (e.g., 49.99)

#### Optional But Recommended:
- **Banner Image**: Upload a hero image (1920x800px works best)
- **Features**: Add 3-5 key features
- **Gallery Images**: Upload 3-8 product screenshots
- **Meta Title**: For better SEO
- **Currency**: Select your preferred currency

3. Click **"Create Landing Page"**

### Step 3: View Your Landing Page
1. After creation, you'll see a success message with the URL
2. The URL will be something like: `/landing/my-awesome-product`
3. Click **"View Page"** or copy the URL
4. Share with customers!

## 💡 Pro Tips

### For Best Results:
1. **Use High-Quality Images**
   - Banner: At least 1920x800px
   - Gallery: At least 800x600px
   - Keep file sizes under 500KB

2. **Write Compelling Copy**
   - Start with benefits, not features
   - Use bullet points
   - Keep paragraphs short

3. **Optimize for Conversions**
   - Clear, specific title
   - Price prominently displayed
   - Strong CTAs
   - Social proof if available

## 🛒 How Purchases Work

When a customer clicks "Buy Now":
1. Product is added to cart
2. Redirected to checkout
3. Existing payment gateways are used (Stripe, PayPal, etc.)
4. Order appears in your admin panel
5. Customer receives download access

## 🔧 Quick Edit
To modify your landing page:
1. Go to **Admin → Landing Pages**
2. Click **"Edit"** on the page you want to change
3. Make your changes
4. Click **"Update Landing Page"**

## ❓ Quick FAQ

**Q: How do I change the URL?**
A: The URL is auto-generated from the title. To change it, edit the title and save.

**Q: Can I have multiple landing pages?**
A: Yes! Create as many as you need for different products.

**Q: What payment methods are supported?**
A: All payment gateways configured in your admin panel work automatically.

**Q: Can I see who visited my landing page?**
A: The landing page uses your existing analytics. Orders appear in the admin panel.

**Q: How do I delete a landing page?**
A: Go to Admin → Landing Pages → Click "Delete" → Confirm.
